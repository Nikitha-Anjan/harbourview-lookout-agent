# Harbourview Lookout — Fire Safety & Occupancy Regulation (HVL-REG-001)

*Synthetic document for assessment purposes only.*

## 1. Maximum Occupancy Limits
1.1 The **Observation Deck** has a maximum instantaneous occupancy of **450
persons** at any time.
1.2 The **Indoor Gardens Hall** has a maximum instantaneous occupancy of **300
persons**.
1.3 The **total site** maximum instantaneous occupancy (all zones combined) is
**900 persons**, inclusive of staff.
1.4 Occupancy is measured as persons *currently inside* a zone (entries minus
exits), not cumulative daily visitors.

## 2. Capacity Thresholds & Actions
2.1 At **80%** of any zone limit, staff must post a "Near Capacity" notice and
station an attendant at the entrance.
2.2 At **95%** of any zone limit, **entry to that zone must be paused** until
occupancy drops below 85%.
2.3 Exceeding **100%** of any zone limit is a **reportable safety incident** and
must be logged with the timestamp and peak count.

## 3. Weather-Related Closures
3.1 The Observation Deck **must close** when sustained wind exceeds **60 km/h**.
3.2 The Observation Deck **must close** during electrical storms (any recorded
lightning within the hour).
3.3 During a deck closure, redirected visitors count toward the **Indoor Gardens
Hall** occupancy and Section 2 thresholds apply.

## 4. Emergency Egress
4.1 All emergency exits must remain unobstructed at all times.
4.2 Evacuation drills must be conducted quarterly and logged.

## 5. Reporting
5.1 Any Section 2.3 incident must be reported to the site manager within 24 hours.
5.2 Monthly occupancy peak reports are required for zones exceeding 80% on any day.

# Harbourview Lookout — Ticketing & Refund Policy (HVL-REG-002)

*Synthetic document for assessment purposes only.*

## 1. Ticket Types
- **Adult** — standard admission.
- **Child** — ages 4–12; children under 4 enter free (no ticket issued).
- **Senior** — ages 65+.
- **Family** — 2 adults + up to 3 children.
- **Annual Pass** — unlimited entry for 12 months.

## 2. Pricing Rules
2.1 Prices may vary by **season** (see HVL-REG-003) and **channel** (online vs.
on-site). Online tickets receive a standing **10% discount** vs. on-site.
2.2 Group bookings of **15+** receive an additional **15% discount**, applied by
staff at time of sale.

## 3. Refund Eligibility
3.1 **Weather closure refund:** If the Observation Deck is closed for the entire
visit window due to weather (per HVL-REG-001 §3), affected ticket holders are
entitled to a **full refund or free re-entry** within 30 days.
3.2 **Partial closure:** If the deck is closed for **more than 2 hours** but not
all day, affected holders are entitled to a **50% refund or free re-entry**.
3.3 **Customer-initiated cancellation:**
   - More than 48h before visit date: **full refund**.
   - 24–48h before: **50% refund**.
   - Less than 24h before or no-show: **no refund**.
3.4 **Annual Passes** are **non-refundable** except within 14 days of purchase if
unused.
3.5 Refunds are processed to the **original payment method** within 10 business
days. On-site cash sales are refunded as store credit unless a manager approves cash.

## 4. Exceptions
4.1 Documented medical emergencies may be granted a full refund at manager
discretion regardless of timing.
4.2 Fraudulent or duplicated tickets are void and non-refundable.

# Harbourview Lookout — Health, Safety & Accessibility (HVL-REG-004)

*Synthetic document for assessment purposes only.*

## 1. Accessibility
1.1 The site must maintain at least **2 operational elevators** to the Observation
Deck during all operating hours; if fewer than 2 are working, staff must offer
assisted access and note it in the daily log.
1.2 Accessible restrooms must be available on every floor.
1.3 Service animals are permitted in all public zones.

## 2. Weather Comfort & Safety
2.1 When outdoor temperature is **≥ 32°C**, free water stations must be deployed
and shaded rest areas opened.
2.2 When outdoor temperature is **≤ -10°C**, outdoor queue areas must be limited
to **15 minutes** and a warming area opened indoors.
2.3 On days with heavy precipitation (**≥ 20 mm**), non-slip mats must be placed
at all entrances.

## 3. Crowd Management
3.1 On days where forecasted or historical foot traffic exceeds **80%** of total
site capacity, an additional **crowd-management briefing** is required at shift start.
3.2 Strollers and large bags are subject to inspection at the Gardens Hall entrance.

## 4. Incident Logging
4.1 All medical incidents, capacity incidents (HVL-REG-001 §2.3), and elevator
outages must be logged in the daily operations record.
4.2 Logs must be retained for **24 months**.

## 5. Food & Beverage Zones
5.1 The Gardens Café must display allergen information for all items.
5.2 Cold-chain items must be held at or below **4°C**; a temperature log is
required twice daily.

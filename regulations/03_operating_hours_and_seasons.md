# Harbourview Lookout — Operating Hours & Seasonal Rules (HVL-REG-003)

*Synthetic document for assessment purposes only.*

## 1. Seasons
- **Peak Season:** June 1 – September 30.
- **Shoulder Season:** April 1 – May 31, and October 1 – October 31.
- **Off Season:** November 1 – March 31.

## 2. Operating Hours
| Season | Mon–Fri | Sat–Sun & Holidays |
|---|---|---|
| Peak | 09:00 – 21:00 | 08:00 – 22:00 |
| Shoulder | 10:00 – 18:00 | 09:00 – 20:00 |
| Off | 10:00 – 16:00 | 10:00 – 17:00 |

2.1 Last entry is **60 minutes** before closing.
2.2 The site is **closed December 25** and **January 1**.

## 3. Seasonal Pricing Multipliers
Base prices are defined for Shoulder season. Apply the multiplier by season:
- Peak: **×1.25**
- Shoulder: **×1.00**
- Off: **×0.80**

## 4. Staffing Baseline (guidance, not mandatory)
4.1 Minimum **1 attendant per 150 expected visitors** during operating hours.
4.2 At least **2 staff** must be on-site whenever the attraction is open.
4.3 During Peak weekends, a **dedicated capacity monitor** is required for the
Observation Deck.

## 5. Holidays (observed)
New Year's Day, Family Day (3rd Mon Feb), Good Friday, Victoria Day, Canada Day,
Labour Day, Thanksgiving (2nd Mon Oct), Christmas Day. Holiday hours follow the
Sat–Sun column.
